How ordering works

Follow these steps in this order for a made-to-measure garment.

  1. Visit the shop and choose your style. Photos are welcome.
  2. Bring your cloth, or choose some from our stock.
  3. We take your measurements and agree a price and a ready date.
  4. Pay half the price to start the work.
  5. Come back for a fitting if the garment needs one.
  6. Collect your clothes and pay the rest.

A simple garment usually takes five to seven days. Allow two to three weeks around Eid, Tabaski and the wedding season.
