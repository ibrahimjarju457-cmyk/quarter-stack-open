# AI Log

A record of how AI was used on this project.

- **Tool:** Claude (Anthropic), via the Claude chat app
- **Date:** 19 October 2026
- **Project:** A one-page services site for a small local business (a tailor in Kerr Serign, The Gambia)

## What I asked and what the AI did

| # | My request | What the AI produced |
|---|------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Make `services.html` for a small business with no web presence: one `<h1>`, an intro paragraph, at least three `<h2>` sections, an `<ol>`, a `<ul>`, and a nested list | `services.html` with inline CSS: invented business, services and prices, and a justification for each list type |
| 2 | Separate the HTML and CSS | Moved the styles into `styles.css` and linked it from `services.html`. No content or design changes |


## What the AI wrote

- All the page copy, including the business name, intro, service names and prices
- The HTML structure and the list choices
- The CSS (colours, spacing, light and dark mode)

## What is not real

- **The business is a placeholder.** "Serign Kunda Tailoring" is invented. The AI could not check for a real shop or ask an owner for permission. The name of the shop is Njie's tailoring shop. 
- **The prices, opening hours, turnaround times and "over ten years" claim are made up.** They are plausible guesses, not facts.
- **The type of clothing the shop produce/ sew.** "The tailor primarly sews west african cloths but can also sew different type of cloths
