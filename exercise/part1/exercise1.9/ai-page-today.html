What we do

We make clothes to measure and mend the ones you already own. Bring your own fabric or choose from what we have in the shop.

  • Kaftans & boubousMade to your measurements for everyday wear, Friday prayers and celebrations.
  • Women’s dresses & African print outfitsSkirts, tops, gowns and matching sets in the style you choose.
  • Men’s shirts, trousers & suitsA proper fit for work, weddings and special occasions.
