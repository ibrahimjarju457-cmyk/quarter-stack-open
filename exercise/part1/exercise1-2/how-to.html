How to register for your courses at civle service university

Before you go

Most registration problems start before you reach the office. Spend a day preparing so that you do not have to make the trip twice.

Check the registration dates

Registration only works inside a set period. Read the notice board or ask your department head when it opens and closes, because arriving late can mean extra delays or a penalty.

Settle your fees first

The office will usually ask for proof that your fees are paid before it registers you. Pay at any Zentih bank, and keep the receipt safe.

What to bring

Bring the originals and a spare copy of each, so the office can keep one.

  • Your student ID card
  • Your fee payment receipt
  • Your course registration form, filled in
  • Your academic transcript
  • Your grade 12 results

At the office

Go early, because queues are shortest in the morning. Hand over your form and receipt, and check that every course on the form is the one you intend to take.

After

Ask for a stamped or signed copy of your registration and keep it until the semester ends. If a course is missing from your record later, this copy is your proof.
